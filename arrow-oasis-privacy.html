Arrow Oasis: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Arrow Oasis is a puzzle game for Android made by OpticalWorks ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

In short: you don't need an account to play, and we don't run any servers that collect your data. Your progress and settings stay on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional purchases through Google Play; those Google services process some information as described below.

1. Information kept on your device

Your progress, settings, playlists and choices (including your consent choices) are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Some pictures the game draws are kept in its cache so it starts faster. None of this is sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android's settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. To show ads, count them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software in the game may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (such as your country or region);
  • information about your device and the game, such as the phone model, Android version, language and game version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as which ads were shown and tapped.

Depending on where you live and the choices you make, Google may use this information to show you personalized ads, or only non-personalized or limited ads.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. The form lists the purposes and Google's advertising partners. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • United States: where state privacy laws give you the right, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or limit personalized ads, in Android's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Google's use of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases, such as Supporter and the full themes, are made through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game which items you own so it can unlock them on your device. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Ratings

The game may ask whether you'd like to rate it, using Google Play's own rating window. Google handles your rating and review; the game is not told whether or how you rated it.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for an account, your name or your email address.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking of our own, and no crash-reporting service.
  • The game doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We don't sell your personal information ourselves.

6. Children

Arrow Oasis is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

7. Keeping and deleting information

What the game saves on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. We hold no copy of it. Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is kept and deleted according to Google's own policies; you can manage it through your Google account and your device's settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or state laws in the United States), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw your consent at any time (Settings → Privacy choices) and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we don't keep personal information on our own systems, most requests are best handled through the in-game choices and Google's tools above, but you can always contact us and we will help.

Where the GDPR applies, personalized advertising and storing or reading information on your device rely on your consent; some ad measurement and fraud prevention by Google and its partners may rely on legitimate interests, as explained in the consent form; processing a purchase relies on our contract with you.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at its top.
